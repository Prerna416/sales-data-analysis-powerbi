# 📊 ElectroHub Sales Data Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed for ElectroHub to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard focuses on product performance, sales trends, profitability, quantity sold, discounts, orders, customer/order details, and city-wise sales.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

- What are the Top 5 and Bottom 5 products based on Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold?
- How do sales trends change over daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual periods?
- What is the relationship between Sales and Profit?
- How do Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold compare between two selected periods?
- What is the average discount offered in each discount category?
- What is the total number of orders?
- How can individual orders be analyzed using filters such as Product, Date, Customer ID, and Promotion Category?
- How do sales vary across different cities?

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

Provides a high-level view of sales performance and important business metrics through interactive visualizations.

### 2. Top & Bottom 5 Analysis

Analyzes the best- and worst-performing products based on:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity Sold

### 3. Sales, Profit & Quantity Comparison

Allows users to compare Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold between selected periods.

### 4. Interactive Analysis

Provides interactive analysis using slicers and visual interactions to explore the sales data from different perspectives.

### 5. Detailed Table

Provides detailed order-level information and allows users to filter the data using relevant business fields.
